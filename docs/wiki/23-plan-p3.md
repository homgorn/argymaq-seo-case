## 38. GEO

| Действие | Как |
|---|---|
| `llms.txt` | создать: список страниц, описание, контакты |
| Структурированный FAQ | на страницах туров |
| Упоминания на VC / Medium | статьи о конных турах в Кыргызстане |
| Reddit | r/Kyrgyzstan, r/travel, r/horses — экспертные ответы |
| Wikipedia / WikiTravel | упоминание Каракола как точки конных туров |

Пример `llms.txt`:

```
# ARGYMAQ KYRGYZSTAN
> Horseback riding tours from Karakol, Kyrgyzstan. 1 to 5 day routes to Altyn Arashan, Ala-Kul lake and Karakol gorge.

- [Our tours](https://argymaq.kg/our-tours/): 1, 2, 4, 5 day horseback tours with prices
- [1-day Karakol gorge](https://argymaq.kg/1-day-horseback-riding-tour-in-karakol-gorge/)
- [2-day Altyn Arashan](https://argymaq.kg/2-day-horseback-riding-itinerary-altyn-arashan/)
- [4-day Ala-Kul](https://argymaq.kg/4-day-horseback-riding-tour-program/)
- [5-day tour](https://argymaq.kg/5-day-horseback-riding-tour-program/)
- [Guesthouse and yurt camp](https://argymaq.kg/guesthouse-and-yurt-camp/)
- [Airport pickup](https://argymaq.kg/airport-pickup/)
- [Contacts](https://argymaq.kg/contacts/): +996 501 282 030, Karakol, Kyrgyzstan
```
