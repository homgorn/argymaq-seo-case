**Niche:** horseback tours, horse riding, guesthouse / yurt camp, transfers

## 1. Executive summary

The site is alive, returns 200, HTTPS certificate valid (Let's Encrypt, until 27.12.2026), mirrors merged, robots.txt and sitemap work, 404 returns correctly, favicon present. The technical skeleton is fine.

But from an SEO standpoint the site is "a business card, not a site": 10 content pages for the whole niche, not a single meta description, not a single schema.org block, not a single analytics counter, images uncompressed (408 MB across 54 files, one file 15.5 MB), and the commercial block (prices, dates, "what is included", booking form) is completely absent. Meanwhile competitors in the Karakol niche already have filled descriptions, Open Graph and prices.

**Main conclusion:** the problem is not a penalty and not technical breakage - the site simply does not give search engines and people what they are looking for. Task number one is not to "catch up" with competitors on formal metrics, but to build a working commercial site with 10-15 target pages instead of the current 3-4 tours without prices.

### Top-5 critical problems

| # | Problem | Fact | Cost of inaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Images not optimised** | 54 PNG = **408 MB**, average 7.7 MB; on the 5-day tour page **150 MB** of images; no webp/avif; 22 files heavier than 10 MB | LCP never enters the green zone on any connection |
| 2 | **Zero schema.org markup** | Checked 15 URLs: not a single `application/ld+json` | No chance at rich results; neural networks do not understand structure |
| 3 | **Zero analytics** | Metrika, GA4, GTM, pixels - nothing on 14 pages | The owner sees neither traffic nor conversions |
| 4 | **Zero meta descriptions** | 0 of 14 pages | The snippet in SERPs is generated from random text |
| 5 | **No commercial block on tour pages** | No price, no dates, no "what is included", no form; contact is WhatsApp only | The visitor cannot decide and leaves |

### Quick wins (1-2 days of work)

- Write 14 meta descriptions (template + toponym + USP).
- Install Yandex Metrika + GA4 via GTM.
- Recompress the 10 heaviest images - minus ~350 MB of traffic.
- Replace `http://` with `https://` in the media library (mixed content).
- Delete the demo post "Hello world" and its comment.
- Add JSON-LD `Organization` + `LocalBusiness` + `TouristTrip`.
- Add `alt` to 54 images.

### Conclusions from the benchmark

1. **Description:** argymaq has 0% filled descriptions (like CBT Karakol). 85% of competitors write them.
2. **Open Graph:** argymaq 0%, competitors 85%. In messengers and social networks a link to argymaq looks bare, competitors get a card with image and description.
3. **JSON-LD:** 0% for everyone - **an open opportunity**: by implementing schema.org first in the niche, argymaq gains an advantage in rich results and in neural-network understanding of structure.
4. **Prices:** 85% of competitors show prices, argymaq does not.
5. **Speed:** argymaq (5.1 s) is roughly equal to the competitor average (5.5 s) - everyone is slow, argymaq is not worse than the market. But 5 s is still poor by Core Web Vitals.
6. **HTML:** argymaq (99.6 KB) is lighter than the competitor average (236.9 KB) - the single technical advantage.
7. **H1:** competitors in the sample show H1 = 0 (Elementor/WPBakery draws headings with divs) - this is a parsing artefact, not a fact of absence. argymaq H1 markup is correct.

## 9. Questions for the owner

1. **Language strategy:** is the site aimed at foreign tourists (EN), locals (KY / RU) or both? This determines the URL structure and hreflang.
2. **Who decides:** do you sell direct or through OTAs (GetYourGuide, KKday, Viator)?
3. **Prices:** ready to publish prices on the site? If not - a calculator or an enquiry form?
4. **Booking:** online payment or is a WhatsApp / Telegram request enough?
5. **Access:** do you have Yandex Webmaster, Google Search Console, Yandex Metrika, Google Analytics? If so - exports for 3-6 months.
6. **Hosting:** where is the site hosted (own VPS, shared hosting, Russian or foreign)?
7. **Who maintains the site:** is there a developer who can edit the theme and nginx, or does the owner make changes through the admin panel?
8. **Photos:** are the originals (RAW / JPG) available for recompression? Judging by `IMG_7365-convert.io_.png`, an online converter was already used.
9. **Reviews:** are there customer reviews that can be published (with permission)?
10. **Business registration:** is there a legal entity / sole proprietor, details for the offer and privacy policy?
11. **.kg domain:** who is it registered to, do you have access to the registrar panel?
12. **Seasonality:** year-round or May-October only?
13. **Budget and timeline:** what budget for the site rework and what deadline?
14. **Analytics:** if Metrika is missing - do you agree to install it?
15. **2GIS / Yandex Maps:** is there an organisation card?
16. **Content:** who will write the texts for the pages?
17. **Translation:** is the Chinese market needed?
18. **The `?p=N` duplicates:** Elementor links or manual?
19. **SSL:** is Let's Encrypt auto-renewal configured?
20. **What has been tried:** which SEO actions were already performed and with what result?
